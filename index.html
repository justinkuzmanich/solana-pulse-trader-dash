How to read this dashboard

The timeframe switch (top right) changes everything on the page at once. 5M answers "what's happening this second," 1H is the working view, 6H shows the session, 1D shows the day. If 5M runs hot while 1D runs cold, the market is just waking up.

Market Heat rolls everything into one 0–100 score. Cold / Quiet: few traders, thin volume — moves are slow and exits can be hard. Active: healthy participation, the everyday zone. Hot: well above normal — fast moves, good liquidity. Frenzy: extreme — biggest opportunities, biggest risk of chasing tops.

The center line on each driver bar is "typical" (the 7-day average for this window and time of day). Right of it = busier than usual. Comparing to the same hour matters because weekends and US evenings always run hotter than a Tuesday 4 AM.

Traders on chain is the most honest signal: volume can be inflated by a few whales, but thousands of distinct fee-paying wallets can't. The heartbeat's dashed lines mark slow, typical, busy and frenzy throughput so the animation reads at a glance.

Tokens created vs migrated is the launchpad funnel. Creations show attention pouring in; migrations show which launches attracted real buyers and graduated to a full DEX where everyone can trade them. Lots of creations with a falling migration rate = a spray of low-quality launches. Migrations rising = money is actually sticking.
